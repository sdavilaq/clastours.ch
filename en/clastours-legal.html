Article 02
Services offered

Clas Tours by Davila Quiroz offers guided hikes in the Swiss Alps and surrounding areas for Spanish-speaking groups. The services include:

  • Hiking guide in Spanish throughout the activity
  • Planning and route selection according to the group's level
  • Pre-trip guidance on equipment, logistics and transport
  • Assistance and communication throughout the day

Not included in the price: transport to the starting point, meals, drinks, accommodation, entrance or access fees, rental of personal equipment, and the client's personal accident insurance.

Kandersteg with a hut night: the Clas Tours price covers only the guiding service on both days. Accommodation and half board at the Blüemlisalphütte (approx. CHF 95 per adult; CHF 80 for SAC members; the hut's 2026 rates, subject to change) are booked and paid by each client directly with the hut. Clas Tours only coordinates the booking, in the name and on behalf of each client: it neither resells nor charges for the accommodation, and the hut's own terms apply to that booking, including those on cancellation and no-show, as well as its supplements (for example for gluten-free or lactose-free menus). Clas Tours does not offer package travel.

Departures take place on demand and with a minimum of 2 participants; the date is agreed with the group.

The tours offered have a maximum rating of T3 (mountain hiking) in summer and WT2 (winter hiking) in winter, according to the difficulty scale of the Swiss Alpine Club (SAC / CAS).

Programme changes: if the activity is not feasible as planned (weather, snow or terrain conditions, the state of the group, closed access), Clas Tours may change the route, time or date, or propose an equivalent alternative in the same area. Reaching a summit or a specific goal is not guaranteed: what is owed is diligent guiding, not a result.

Substitution: if Clas Tours cannot lead the activity in person because of illness, accident or another justified reason, it may entrust it to another qualified guide, informing the client as soon as possible.

Article 06
Participation and health requirements

The client is responsible for honestly assessing their physical and technical condition before booking. Clas Tours may request information about the participant's experience level and reserves the right to recommend a different tour or deny participation if it considers that the safety of the client or the group could be compromised.

The client answers the questions on the form and from the guide about experience, fitness and health completely and truthfully, and follows the safety instructions during the activity (duty to cooperate). False or withheld information may lead to exclusion without a refund.

General requirements:

  • Good general fitness suited to the level of the selected tour
  • Suitable hiking footwear (mandatory for T2 and T3 tours)
  • Clothing and equipment appropriate for the weather conditions
  • Ability to walk for at least 4 continuous hours on uneven terrain
  • Under-18s: must be accompanied by a responsible adult

The client declares that they do not suffer from medical conditions that could be aggravated by mountain hiking (severe heart problems, pronounced vertigo, serious breathing difficulties, etc.) without having informed the guide beforehand. If in doubt, consult your doctor before taking part.

Clas Tours reserves the right to exclude any participant who arrives at the meeting point intoxicated by alcohol or other substances, with no right to a refund.

Article 08
Insurance

The service price does not include personal accident insurance or mountain rescue insurance for the client.

It is strongly recommended that all participants have:

  • Personal accident insurance covering mountain hiking activities
  • Mountain rescue insurance (in Switzerland, Rega — the Swiss Air Rescue — offers annual patron memberships)
  • Travel insurance covering medical evacuation (especially for participants who do not live in Switzerland)
  • Cancellation-cost insurance (especially advisable for the route with a night in a hut)
  • European Health Insurance Card (for EU/EFTA citizens) or international health insurance for everyone else

The costs of rescue, medical evacuation, hospitalisation and repatriation are borne by the participant or their insurer, unless the damage is due to fault on the part of Clas Tours.

A mountain rescue in Switzerland can cost between CHF 1,000 and CHF 10,000 or more. Don't set out without insurance.
