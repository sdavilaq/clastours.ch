About this tour

The most complete experience I offer. Two days in the heart of the Bernese Oberland. On the first day you climb through the Griesalp valley to the Blüemlisalphütte. On the second, the descent past Lake Oeschinensee to Kandersteg is postcard-perfect — one of the most beautiful lakes in Switzerland. Read the complete guide to the crossing →

Griesalp → Blüemlisalphütte

Blüemlisalphütte → Oeschinensee → Kandersteg
Early breakfast at the hut. Panoramic descent to Lake Oeschinensee. Continue to Kandersteg.

About this tour

The "Swiss Grand Canyon". A rocky amphitheatre almost 160 metres high in the heart of the Neuchâtel Jura, home to a colony of ibex that is often seen on the ledges and on the trail. Spectacular in every season. Perfect for your first big hike in Switzerland. Read the complete Creux du Van guide →

4 to 6h walking · Pace adapted to the group

A ridge that leaves you speechless. From the Augstmatthorn along the whole crest to the Harderkulm, with Lake Thun, Lake Brienz and the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau in the background. You finish in Interlaken. Demanding and absolutely epic.

About this tour

A unique day combining two icons of Swiss industry: the art of precision watchmaking at the Musée Longines and the pleasure of artisan chocolate at the Camille Bloch factory. In between, a relaxed walk through the green Suze river valley in the Bernese Jura. Perfect for everyone — no technical difficulty, plenty of character.

Itinerary
1
Visit Musée Longines · Saint-Imier
We start at the Musée Longines, with more than 150 years of watchmaking history. Free entry, advance booking required. Santiago guides you through the visit in Spanish.
Hours Mon–Fri · 9am–12pm and 1:30pm–5pm · Free · Booking required
2
Walk through the Suze valley
Flat, well-signposted trail along the valley floor to Courtelary. Peaceful Bernese Jura scenery, ideal for chatting and enjoying without rushing.
~1h45 walking · Relaxed pace
3
Visit Camille Bloch factory · Courtelary
End of the stage at the factory of the famous Ragusa. Shop, exhibition and optionally a guided tour of the production process.
Tue–Sun · 9:30am–6pm · Entry CHF 11 adults / CHF 7 children · Pay at the ticket desk
